Thomas Prommer is Group CEO of We The Flywheel, the AI-native digital group company he founded in 2019. The group spans Flywheel Media, a performance media network of 700+ properties across 42 markets, along with Flywheel Studio, Flywheel Motion, Flywheel Sports and Flywheel Advisory. Based in New York, he works as a CTO/CIO and AI strategy consultant. He was previously Global SVP Engineering at adidas (2020–2022), President, Technology at Huge, and Chief Information Officer at sweetgreen.

In 2026 he was named to the CTO Craft 100, named Global CIO 2026 by HotTopics, and named “Best Enterprise AI Strategy Leader 2026” by The Enterprise World, which featured him as a cover story. He founded ctaio.dev, a community for the next-gen tech executive, and aistrategy.guide, on enterprise AI strategy. His books, The Compounding CEO and The AI Marketing Flywheel (with Andi Cross), are due 1 March 2027. Outside work, he is a hybrid athlete.
